Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026 · App: Dodam (도담)
Language: English · 한국어 · 日本語 · 中文 · Tiếng Việt

Article 1 (Purpose of Processing)

Dodam (도담, the "App") handles information for the following purposes.

  • Recording your child's growth and showing it on growth charts
  • Showing the vaccination, checkup and paperwork schedule for children in Korea, and reminding you of it
  • Showing your own photos by your baby's age in months, and making age-stamped photos, postcards, photobooks and growth movies
  • Helping you fill in the vaccination screening form and describe symptoms at the hospital (hospital card)
  • Processing the one-time in-app purchase (Dodam Plus)

Article 2 (Information Stored on Your Device)

The App works without sign-up or login. We do not operate any server, and the App has no ads, analytics or crash reporting. While running, the App does not use the internet except for the app store's own purchase processing. Everything you enter is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

👶
Child information
Your child's name, date of birth, sex (optional) and nationality (optional, used only to show the paperwork that applies).
📏
Growth and schedule records
Height, weight and head circumference measurements, marks for completed schedule items, and "Firsts" milestones.
📝
Screening-form helper answers
Answers you keep in the vaccination screening-form helper. The helper never stores names or ID numbers; you write those on the paper form yourself.
🖼️
Photo references and settings
IDs of photos you hid or picked for Firsts and the photobook (with their capture time and file name, so they can be found again), and your app settings. The photos themselves are not copied into the App.

Article 3 (Device Permissions)

The App asks for the following permissions only when you use the related feature. You can change them at any time in your device settings.

  • Photos (read): to show your own photos grouped by your baby's age in months. Photos are read on the device and are never transmitted. On iOS, the App also works with limited access (only the photos you select).
  • Photos (add): to save the age-stamped photos, postcards, photobook pages and growth movies you make to your photo library.
  • Notifications: for reminders about vaccinations, checkup periods, paperwork deadlines and anniversaries. Reminders are scheduled on the device, and there is no push server. You can turn them off in the App's settings or in your device settings.
  • Files: "Export records" creates a backup file and saves it wherever you choose. "Restore from file" reads that file back. The file contains your child's information, growth records, done marks, Firsts, screening-form answers and settings (photo IDs only, not the photos themselves).

Article 4 (In-App Purchase)

Dodam Plus is a one-time (non-consumable) purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

The App does not receive your payment card information. Whether you own Plus is checked on the device with the store, and we do not operate any receipt server.

An invite code (for teachers) can also unlock Plus. The code is checked on the device and is not sent anywhere.

Apple Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Article 5 (Provision to Third Parties)

We do not collect your information, so we do not provide it to third parties.

The only outside processing is the following:

  • Purchases: payment for Dodam Plus is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies (see Article 4)
  • Hospital card voice: Korean audio pre-generated and bundled in the App, with the device's built-in speech engine as a fallback; the App itself sends nothing externally (how the device speech engine works follows the device maker and OS policies)

Article 6 (International Transfer)

The App is released only in Korea. Purchase processing by the following companies may take place outside Korea:

  • Apple Inc. (United States): payment processing for Dodam Plus on iOS
  • Google LLC (United States): payment processing for Dodam Plus on Android

Article 7 (Retention and Deletion)

Everything the App stores stays on your device. It is deleted when you use Settings → "Start over" or delete the App.

Backup files you exported stay where you saved them and remain under your control. Please delete them yourself when you no longer need them.

Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Article 8 (Your Rights)

You can view, change or delete your information at any time.

  • Edit records: directly on each screen of the App
  • Delete all on-device data: Settings → "Start over", or delete the App
  • Exported files: delete them from where you saved them
  • Photo and notification access: change it in your device settings

When you move to a new device, use "Restore from file" to bring your records and "Restore purchase" on the Dodam Plus screen to bring your purchase.

Article 9 (Children's Information)

The App is meant to be used by parents and guardians. Information about the child is entered by the parent or guardian and stays on the device.

We do not collect information from children.
